# Demo Plan — Recruitment Lead Presentation

## Demo promise

Show not just a vacancy post, but a full mini-campaign pipeline:

**real industrial environment → privacy-safe sketch style → strong Hebrew lead → multilingual recruitment package**

## Recommended demonstration structure

### 1. Opening frame — “This is not a standard job ad”

Message:

> לא עוד מודעת דרושים. זה הלב האנרגטי של המפעל.

Purpose: immediately separate the work from generic HR posters.

Visual: monochrome sketch of boiler / compressor / pipe room, dark overlay, one strong accent color.

### 2. Before / after transformation

Show:
- raw industrial reference stays local;
- stylized sketch/illustration becomes the public-facing visual;
- identifiers are removed or blurred.

Purpose: demonstrate creative + privacy-aware workflow.

### 3. Main Hebrew lead

Use master headline:

> הלב האנרגטי של המפעל מחפש את האנשים הבאים שלו

Supporting line:

> מדחסים • צ׳ילרים • דוודים • תשתיות אנרגיה

Purpose: position the role as meaningful technical work.

### 4. Candidate-fit frame

Message:

- הנדסאים עם ניסיון
- הנדסאים צעירים שרוצים ללמוד
- בעלי ניסיון רלוונטי גם ללא תעודה

Purpose: make the recruitment funnel clear without killing the emotional tone.

### 5. Multilingual expansion

Show the same campaign can be adapted to:
- Hebrew
- English
- Arabic
- Russian
- Hindi / exact Indian language TBD

Purpose: demonstrate scale and audience reach.

## Best format for the demo

### Option A — 5-slide presentation / carousel

1. Hero: energy heart of the factory
2. Systems: compressors, chillers, boilers, energy infrastructure
3. Who we need
4. Multilingual preview
5. CTA / contact

This is the recommended first deliverable.

### Option B — short vertical video / reel

Use slow pans / zooms over sketch images with large Hebrew text overlays.

Structure:
- 0–2 sec: “לא עוד מודעת דרושים”
- 2–6 sec: “הלב האנרגטי של המפעל...”
- 6–10 sec: systems list
- 10–14 sec: who we need
- 14–18 sec: CTA

### Option C — one premium poster

Strong single image, useful for WhatsApp / Facebook / LinkedIn, but weaker for demonstrating the whole capability.

## Visual direction

Use the new sketch-style images as the core style:
- black/white industrial drawing
- high contrast
- one accent color: electric yellow / warm orange / Strauss red
- text blocks over dark translucent panels
- no readable equipment labels or logos

## Privacy checklist before publishing

- remove/blur logos, especially equipment brand marks when visible;
- remove/blur serial numbers, panel text, screens, signs;
- avoid publishing raw media;
- use stylized exports only;
- review final at high zoom.
