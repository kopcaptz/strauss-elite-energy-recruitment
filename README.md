# Strauss / Elite Energy Recruitment Lead

Demo-quality multilingual recruitment campaign for the energy/infrastructure department at Strauss / Elite, Nof HaGalil.

## Start here

Goal: create a “wow” recruiting lead, not a dry HR vacancy.

Core idea:

> The energy heart of the factory is looking for its next people.

Visual world:
- boiler room / energy node: boilers, pipes, red burners, yellow service platforms;
- compressor / machine room: Atlas Copco unit, ducts, yellow equipment, operational humming;
- real industrial scale, clean and serious, not staged office HR.

Languages planned:
- Hebrew — master version first
- English
- Arabic
- Russian
- Hindi / another Indian language — clarify exact language later
