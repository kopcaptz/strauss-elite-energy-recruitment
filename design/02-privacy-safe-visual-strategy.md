# Privacy-Safe Visual Strategy

## Working idea

Use the real factory photos/videos only as internal visual references, then transform them into stylized illustrated/graphic assets so the final campaign does not look like direct workplace photography.

This can reduce risk and make the campaign look more premium, but it does **not** automatically solve all confidentiality / workplace approval questions. Treat it as a safer creative direction, not a legal shield.

## Recommended direction

Create visuals that preserve the *feeling* of the equipment, not exact identifying details:

- industrial energy room
- boilers / compressors / chillers / pipes
- red/yellow technical accents
- clean high-tech factory utility environment
- no readable labels, serial numbers, screens, maps, badges, faces, reflections, or unique layout markers

## Safer asset workflow

1. Start from real photos as local reference only.
2. Crop out identifying details.
3. Remove/blur logos, labels, serials, screens, documents, badge areas.
4. Convert to an illustration style:
   - technical poster
   - cinematic industrial illustration
   - blueprint / schematic poster
   - vector-like recruitment graphic
   - semi-realistic digital painting
5. Review final image at 100–200% zoom for hidden identifiers.
6. Only upload/share the sanitized final export, not the raw photos.

## Visual styles to test

### A. Industrial poster illustration

Strong recruitment-poster look, dramatic lighting, pipes and machinery simplified, no brand labels.

### B. Blueprint / energy-map style

Dark navy background, white/cyan linework, yellow/red highlights, abstracted compressors/boilers/chillers.

### C. Semi-realistic digital painting

Looks inspired by a real machinery room but not like an exact photo. Good balance between credibility and safety.

### D. Isometric technical room

Stylized isometric energy room with compressors, boilers, chillers, piping. Very safe because layout is invented.

## Prompt seed

Create a premium industrial recruitment poster illustration inspired by a factory energy infrastructure room: compressors, boilers, chillers, pipes, ducts, control panels, warm red and yellow technical accents, clean Israeli manufacturing environment, cinematic lighting, professional engineering atmosphere, no people, no logos, no readable text, no serial numbers, no exact real-world identifiers, not photorealistic, high-end technical illustration.
